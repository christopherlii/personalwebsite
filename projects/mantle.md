mantle is a desktop app that watches how you work and suggests what to do next. [ben](https://www.linkedin.com/in/benjxu/) and i built it at Tech@NYU's buildathon, and won best use of Nia.

## why

every AI assistant sees the prompt in front of it and nothing else, so you keep re-explaining your own context. we wanted one that already knew.

## how it works

it reads your screen as you work, pulls out the people, projects, and topics involved, and builds a graph of how they connect — keeping what you return to, letting the rest fade. press Alt+K and it offers a few next steps for whatever's on screen: draft a reply, summarize this, look something up.
